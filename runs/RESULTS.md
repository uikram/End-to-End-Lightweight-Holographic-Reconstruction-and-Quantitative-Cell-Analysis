## What this document does not establish

- **Segmentation labels are circular.** Every mask is `Otsu(gaussian * phase_reference)`, so segmentation is scored against a threshold of its own input. Independent labels are needed before any Dice or recall here is a statement about the world.
- **The forward-model term is a diagnostic, not a loss.** A 10% phase error lowers its residual, so its gradient points the wrong way near the truth. Measured in `runs/z_calibration.json` and figure 16; see `docs/documentation.md` section 6.2a.
- **The amplitude reference is a reconstruction, not a measurement.** Any amplitude result is agreement with one algorithm's output.
